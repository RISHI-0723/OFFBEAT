# OFFBEAT Product Specification

## Product statement
OFFBEAT is a community-powered discovery economy that rewards useful recommendations about independent artists, cultural experiences, and overlooked creators—not merely popularity.

## Tagline
Discover differently. Reward discovery.

## Problem
Social platforms optimize for engagement and popularity. Curators who surface valuable but overlooked creators often receive little recognition, while organizers may struggle to measure whether promotion led to meaningful participation.

## Solution
A focused platform where:
- curators recommend creators/events and explain why they matter;
- community members discover, save, and act on recommendations;
- event organizers validate eligible participation;
- a transparent scoring model estimates contribution;
- sponsors fund pools and distribute rewards;
- an impact ledger makes allocations inspectable.

## Target users
1. Curators: people with strong taste, local knowledge, or niche expertise.
2. Creators: independent artists, musicians, makers, and cultural organizers.
3. Community members: people looking for trusted recommendations.
4. Sponsors/organizers: groups that fund discovery and want transparent reporting.

## Main user journeys
### Curator
1. Open discovery feed.
2. Submit a recommendation with title, creator, category, link, artwork, and explanation.
3. View recommendation status, eligible actions, contribution score, and reward history.
4. Build reputation through consistent, useful contributions.

### Participant
1. Open a recommendation.
2. View the curator's reason and creator/event details.
3. Follow the event link or open event details.
4. Check in using a one-time, expiring event token in the demo.
5. See confirmation and the impact ledger update.

### Sponsor/organizer
1. Create a campaign/pool with a title, budget, eligibility, and end date.
2. Fund a pool using testnet MON if the contract integration is live.
3. View recommendations, verified actions, and score explanations.
4. Approve a distribution round.
5. View onchain transaction and allocation history.

## MVP screens
- Landing/discovery feed
- Recommendation detail
- Submit recommendation
- Creator/event detail
- Curator dashboard
- Sponsor dashboard
- Impact ledger
- Wallet connect/status (only if functional)
- Judge/demo instructions

## Non-goals
- Full social network, direct messages, follower graph, or comments system.
- Launching a tradable token.
- Mainnet or real-money rewards.
- Complex AI ranking.
- Fully decentralized governance.
- Claiming perfect attribution or fraud prevention.

## Success metrics for a demo/pilot
- Number of recommendations submitted.
- Unique curators.
- Eligible unique check-ins.
- Check-in validation/rejection rate.
- Reward calculation correctness.
- Successful testnet funding/allocation/claim transactions.
- Repeat curator activity in a later pilot.

## Trust and transparency
- Show how every score is calculated.
- Label seeded/demo content.
- Show pending, eligible, rejected, and rewarded statuses.
- Explain that referral evidence is not causal proof.
- Do not display invented metrics as real outcomes.
